How your grade is determined

Your grade combines problem sets (40%), two lab reports (20%), a midterm exam (15%), a cumulative final exam (15%), and participation (10%). Participation is based on in-class activities across the semester; there is nothing to submit for it.

Problem sets. There are four problem sets, each submitted on ELMS by 11:59 PM: Problem Set 1 on Wednesday, September 16; Problem Set 2 on Wednesday, October 7; Problem Set 3 on Monday, November 2; and Problem Set 4 on Wednesday, December 2. You may work with one classmate, but each of you submits your own write-up.

Lab reports. Lab Report A, on descriptive statistics for a dataset of your choice, is due September 30. Lab Report B, on hypothesis testing, is due two weeks after Lab Report A.

Exams. The midterm exam is given in class during Week 8. The final exam is cumulative; its date and room are TBA by the registrar.

Other important dates

These are not graded. The last day to drop without a W is September 9. There is no class on Monday, September 7 (Labor Day) or on Wednesday, November 25. Please read Chapter 2 of OpenIntro Statistics before September 14.

Policies

Extensions are granted for documented emergencies. Contact the instructor before the deadline whenever possible. Academic integrity rules apply to all submitted work; cite any code or text you did not write.
